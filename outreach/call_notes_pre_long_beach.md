## Partner offer, as explained on the call

- Round Partner: credits plus an exclusive short-form ad for that round.
- Season Partner: written into the story, placed the way a film places a product (e.g., Rugged Radios on the team radio, GT Radial's rep in the trailer).
- Title Partner: "brought to you by" for one episode, category-exclusive. Example categories: oil, tires, arms (Wisefab, PBM), exhaust.
- Season Owner: category lock across every episode and all social content.
- Every partner gets an ad per round, shot to convert as a paid ad, not cut from the episode. Running the ads is an optional add-on.
- Matt's pushback: Season 3 content was about the story, not individual brands. Brands need a one-for-one example of exactly what they get.
