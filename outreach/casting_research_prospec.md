## License rules (verified)

- **Top 3** in PROSPEC are guaranteed a PRO license if they choose to advance. The other routes are a Hotpit Autofest win or a petition to the series.
- **Below 25th:** drivers may lose their license unless they meet FD's criteria or petition.
- Source: https://news.formulad.com/2026/fd-news/formula-drift-reorganizes-pro-am-affiliate-program-to-assist-new-drivers-obtain-a-competition-license/
- **No one has been confirmed as moving up to PRO for 2027 yet.**

## Flags on the current picks

- **Christian Cotrone (26th).** His PROSPEC license is at risk unless he meets the criteria or petitions. The show's spine depends on him racing in 2027. Confirm his plan.
- **Collete Davis (28th).** Same license risk. She also drives for **Team ENEOS**, an oil brand. Liqui-Moly, a Season 3 incumbent, holds the oil category. If Liqui-Moly buys category exclusivity, an ENEOS car in the episodes is a conflict. The upside is a big audience: about 390K YouTube subscribers, and she hosted TV (TLC's *Girl Starter*). Like Christian, she lives in Orlando.
- **Jerry Johnson (6th).** Solid results, and 2025 Rookie of the Year. He has the same C6 Corvette as Christian and shares sponsors with him (945 Garage, Feal), which is a natural on-screen link. There's little public video of him, so his on-camera candor is unproven. Meet him before committing.

## Best other candidates, ranked for show potential

1. **Jason Webb #45** (@webbdrift45). Right-hand-drive Mustang. A self-funded South African based in Calgary, with a PRO license in hand. The 2027 story is whether he can afford the jump. He vlogs every round and talks openly about money. Small, independent team, so access is high.
2. **Braden Lons #13** (@braden.lons). Age 16, eligible for PRO. He reached the Stafford final with a separated shoulder, and has a running rivalry with Camren Sorensen. He's a minor, so a parent must consent. On-camera candor is unproven.
3. **Camren Sorensen #514** (@camrensorensen). Age 15. Missed the PRO license by 2 points. His brother Branden is 3rd in PRO and his sister Amanda races PROSPEC. He posts openly on YouTube. He's a minor, and his family team has corporate sponsors.
4. **Dmitriy Brutskiy #85** (@dimabrutskiy). Three-time champion who struggled in PRO. Does he try PRO again?
5. **Cory Talaska #116** (@corytalaska). Father-and-son program. Missed promotion two years running.
6. **Tyler Mayer #133** (FD lists @tyler_mayer, his own channel says @Tyler.mayer, so check). A shop owner in his rookie year, and the most candid driver on video. He already has his own videographer.
7. **Chellee Hansen #109** (@chelleehansen). FD's only husband-and-wife team. Her health story needs explicit consent before it's ever mentioned.
8. **Ayden Copham #44** (@ayden.copham). Forsberg Racing protégé. The Nissan-backed team likely controls its media.

Alternates: Kasey Kohl (Vegas runner-up, shop owner), Dustyn Farrell, Sean Booth (Feal car).
