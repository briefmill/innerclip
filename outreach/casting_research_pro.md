# PRO casting research (Oct 14, 2026)

Sources: formulad.com and news.formulad.com round reports, Wrecked Magazine, Drifted, drivers' YouTube channels. No 2027 team or car announcements were found yet. Those will likely come after Long Beach (Oct 23-24).

**How competitiveness is measured here.** Several 2026 rounds had 31 drivers for 32 spots, so "made the main event" says little. The Top 16 rate across the six contested rounds is used instead. Indy was cancelled.

Standings after R7 (preliminary): Conor Shanahan 212, Deane 165, Branden Sorensen 164, Jack Shanahan 162, Bakchis 131, Aasbo 128, Matt Field 119, Hughes 90, Tuerck 87, Forsberg 82, Cole Richards 78, Stuke 57.

## Shortlisted

- **Cole Richards #11** (GT Radial). PRO rookie, 11th, leads Rookie of the Year by 41 points, which isn't clinched until Long Beach.
  - 4th in his PRO debut at Long Beach. Hit the wall at Stafford and failed to qualify. Lost a One More Time at Seattle to Cody Buchanan when his handbrake failed.
  - 2027 car plans unknown. He has an E36 and an E92 M3 in progress on YouTube. Ask him.
- **Ryan Litteral #909** (Kumho).
  - **Weak 2026:** Top 16 once in six rounds.
  - FD driver liaison on the Indy course redesign alongside Matt and Ken Gushi.
  - Reputation for a calm, calculated style, which may read as reserved on camera.
  - Kumho tires conflict with GT Radial if GT Radial buys the tire category.

## Best other candidates, ranked for show potential

1. **Branden Sorensen #513** (GT Radial, privateer BMW E46, @branden_sorensen). 3rd in points, and made the Top 8 at every contested round.
   - Engineer and machinist whose family shop makes his parts.
   - Brother Camren (15) and sister Amanda race PROSPEC. One pit, three storylines, both classes.
   - Blunt vlogs: "This Weekend Tried to End Our Season... I Should've Crashed".
   - Matt knocked him out at his home round in Vegas.
2. **Dylan Hughes #129** (GT Radial, privateer BMW E46, @dylanhughes129). **Strongest link to Matt.**
   - His car landed on top of Matt's Corvette at Utah 2025.
   - He beat Matt at Long Beach 2026.
   - Shares GT Radial and Heatwave with Matt.
   - Former Forsberg crew. His first win (Orlando 2022) came by beating Matt, then Forsberg.
   - Runs his own SoCal shop and posts raw team vlogs.
3. **Cody Buchanan #272** (Kumho, @cbuchanan272). 2025 PROSPEC champion, now a PRO rookie and Cole's Rookie of the Year rival. The two have already had a One More Time.
   - Most candid vlogs of anyone (Garage 272): "Did we get robbed of our first FD Pro win?"
   - Top 16 in 2 of 6 rounds. Kumho.
4. **Jack Shanahan #59** (Kumho, family program from Ireland). 4th in points, Top 16 at every round, chasing his brother Conor (the likely 2026 champion).
   - Their own channel is already big. Conor is Red Bull and JYR, so expect media controls on him.
   - Return for 2027 unconfirmed.
5. **Odi Bakchis #723** (Feal, self-built BRZ). Won Orlando and led the championship, then engine failures dropped him to 5th.
   - Feal is also on the brand list. Decide whether he's a driver, a partner, or both.
6. **Chris Forsberg #64** (Nissan Z). Lost NOS after about 20 years. New title sponsor. Building Ayden Copham's PROSPEC car.
   - Expect Nissan media approval. He runs his own channel.
7. **Dan Stuke #527** (wildcard). Vegas Final 4, where he likely lost to Matt. Little evidence of how he comes across on camera.
